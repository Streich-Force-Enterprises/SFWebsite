  • Voice: plain-spoken, same spirit, but "consulting" comes out (we now sell paid Discovery). Buttons: Request service · Book a discovery call · Tell us what you need.
  • Six small decisions are still open (P1–P6), each with my recommendation. Say "go with all recommended" to lock them. Then C2–C5 can start.

1 · Problem

  • The site presents three equal divisions with generic words ("fix my back office", "view inventory"). It says nothing about what SF Ops and SF Solutions actually do.
  • Your strategy (Step 02) has moved on. The business is now a shared SF backbone: connectors, exception handling and SF Ops as an optional workspace, sold as Discover → Implement → Stabilize → Operate (Step 03). The site doesn't sell any of that.
  • SF Ops is invisible on the site, even though it runs every Streich Force job and is your strongest proof.

Why SF Ops appears on both doors. On Services a facilities buyer experiences SF Ops (quote link, photos, Work summary) before being offered it, which is the easiest sale there is. Voice

  • Retire: "No theory, no consulting, no middlemen." Paid Discovery and Implementation are consulting, so the line would contradict the Solutions page.
  • Draft replacement: "Real work. Real systems. No middlemen." The C-tickets can refine the wording. The tone stays: short sentences, plain words, no buzzwords.
  • Echo your Step 02 principle where it fits: "Keep the systems that work. We connect them."

4 · Guardrails (what the site must not say)

  • No prices. Steps 03 and 04 say pricing is provisional (outgate rates, minimums, discovery fee). Nothing from them goes on the site. The site says "Book a discovery call".
  • No write-back to Triplemeter. SF Solutions is read-only to TM (CLAUDE.md). Step 02's "orchestration / update records" covers the customer's own systems and must never be worded as "we update your TM data".
  • No promises the repos can't keep. AI autonomy levels, WhatsApp intake and cross-customer learning are strategy, not shipped. They stay off the site until sf-solutions or sf-ops has them. "Surfaces exceptions with evidence" is fine once the cockpit shows it.
  • No customer data in screenshots, never Loadstar's. Demo data only (D5).
  • Official names from the SF Ops ontology: Work order, Site, Field tech, Work summary, Client Due.
